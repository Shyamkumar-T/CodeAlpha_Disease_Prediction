## Research reporting guidance

Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al. TRIPOD+AI statement: updated
guidance for reporting clinical prediction models that use regression
or machine learning methods. BMJ. 2024;385:e078378.
https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-078378

This reporting guideline informs the emphasis on transparent methods,
explicit limitations, and clear separation between development and
evaluation. It does not validate this project's model.
